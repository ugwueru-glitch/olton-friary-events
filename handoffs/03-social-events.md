# Handoff 03: Social Events

**Mockup:** `mockups/SocialEvents.dc.html` (computer), `mockups/SocialEventsPhone.dc.html` (phone)

## Layout

The same as handoff 02, with these differences:

- The header bar is warm gold, with dark ink text and back arrow. The title is "Social Events".
- The Week/Month control has a dark ink border, and the selected side is filled gold with dark ink text.
- Arrow buttons are pale gold with dark ink arrows.
- Day headings are dark ink, because gold text is too faint on white.
- The scroll bar is gold on pale gold.
- There is no feast line.

The end-of-list "Last updated" and unofficial lines match handoff 02.

## Behavior

The same as handoff 02.

## What counts as a social event

- Parish hall events, concerts, markets, fayres and fundraising nights.
- Workshops and talks, such as the Christmas Flower Workshops and the Shroud of Turin talk.
- The Bereavement Support Group.

Left out on purpose: the weekly classes under "Regular activities in our parish hall", such as Zumba, Yoga and Slimming World. They would crowd out the one-off events.

## Content rules

- An end time goes in the note, as in "Until 2:00pm. Parish Hall. Tables £15".
- Useful practical details go in the note: place, price and how to sign up.
- With no published time, show "Time to be confirmed".
- The same event from two sources appears once. "HCPT Concert Night" in the newsletter and "Music and Spice Night" in the PDF are one event.
- "OLC" means Our Lady of Compassion.
- Email addresses for booking are left out, because contact details never appear.
