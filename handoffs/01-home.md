## Layout

Top to bottom. Nothing else appears on this screen.

1. **Background:** `assets/church-background.png`, filling the whole screen at 60% strength over white. Crop from the bottom, positioned 20% from the left, so the church stays in view on narrow screens.
2. **Title:** "Olton Friary" in black Gelasio regular, curved in a gentle arc like a rainbow. 80px on computer, 54px on phone.
3. **Gap:** about 80px on computer and 72px on phone.
4. **Buttons:** two large ovals, side by side on computer and stacked on phone.

| | Church Events | Social Events |

| Colour | Friary purple, 85% solid | Warm gold, 85% solid |
