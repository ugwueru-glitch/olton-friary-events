Dear Brothers & Sisters ,
All Saints Day is the day we honour all the
many Saints in heaven who are our role
models & intercessors.
All Souls Day (Monday) its younger
brother has more emotional pull than All
Saints because it concerns those we have
loved who've gone before us.
St Martin of Peru (Tuesday) is loved by the
disadvantaged, especially those who have
known racial abuse. A Dominican lay brother & descendant of slaves, he was the 1st African-American to be declared a saint and is deservedly popular among black people all over the world.
St Charles Borromeo (Wednesday) was
bishop of Milan after the 16th cent Council of Trent & implemented Trent reforms e.g. good education for clergy & laity. He faced opposition to reforms of the Council (much as Pope Leo today re- Vatican II) but faced it down
The Johnson Fund has supported Diocesan
priests for 315 yrs. Priests who are infirm,
sick or retired often depend on support
of the Fund. During this past year the
Association gave grants of over £399,000
to approx. 60 priests. Your generosity is
much valued. Always remember to pray
for work of the Association & members.
St Willibrord (Saturday) was an 8th cent.
Yorkshireman taught in Northumberland
and Ireland. After ordination he went to
evangelise the Friesians becoming the first
Bishop of Utrecht, and founder of monas
teries & dioceses in Denmark Germany & Luxembourg. He died in 739.
The Johnson Fund collection today is for retired Birmingham Diocesan Clergy

Notices
Today is All Saints day when we celebrate all the Saints in heaven, those well-known & those lesser known.
Its little brother All Souls Day tomorrow has perhaps more emotional traction, as it concerns people in our own families that we have lost and still mourn.
Our Annual Celebration of Life Mass takes place on Saturday at 10.15, when we pray for all those in our parish who have died during this past year. This year we are blessed once more with the presence of Bishop David Evans who will preside.
Blessings at all the different cemeteries in the area continue throughout November.
Meanwhile our Friary Bereavement Group meets this afternoon at 3.00pm. If you have suffered any kind of loss, no matter how long ago. You are welcome to join this group as we support each other in our path to new life.
Fr.Francis Joseph Scalpel features in mass intentions for Tuesday. He was a Maltese priest (1895-1970) building up the churchcommunity in Dolgellau North Wales for decades plus a fine building there in 1968.
Our priests have historic connections with Dolgellau, as an older SCJ generation likeFr.Ted, Fr.Dominic and Bro.Michael did their training in nearby Caerdeon Hall.
We served as chaplains to the Dolgellau Carmelite Convent for many many yearsand have happy community memories of
that time in North Wales.
Our Lady of Good Remedies feast on Friday is scarcely known in this country, but is celebrated with gusto in Mexico.
May our Mother Mary be a remedy for all that has gone awry in our lives whether personal or social, and may she watch over us all this week.

SCJ News
Basque Country, France & Spain: Bro. Thang & Fr.Austin visit places associated with our Founder St Michael this week. His first parish was Cambo 20km from Bayonne.There he discovered devotion to the Sacred
Heart of Jesus among people of the parish. It moved him to dedicate our society to the Sacred Heart.
At Cambo is Villa Arnaga , home of Edmond Rostand author of Cyrano de Bergerac.
A historic event occurred here in 2018 when ETA the Basque separatist group agreed to lay down their arms & join democratic politics. British & Irish negotiators witnessed this: Bertie Ahern, Gerry Adams and Jonathan Powell.
Also present..Michel Camdessus, former head of the IMF & pupil of our Betharram school.

BLESSED ARE THOSE WHO MOURN
The Friary bereavement support group is a monthly gathering for anyone seeking support following a loss.
All are welcome, regardless of how long ago the loss, or what kind of loss we face.
We meet 1st Sunday of the month for prayer conversation & encouragement of one another as we continue our grief journey.
We aren't a counselling group but promise good listening skills, respect for confidentiality and of course a cuppa.
Next meeting Sunday 1st November. For this month's meeting please bring a photo of your loved one.
Time : 3pm - 4.30pm, Friary Lounge.

Marriage Matters: All Saints Day
Apocalypse 7:2-4; 9-14 1 John 3:1-3 Matt 5:1-12
Today's Gospel tells us that it is not only when we are strong that we live God's plan for our marriage. Sometimes, when we are weak, our Sacramental way of life is about using our energy to minister to each other. Great joy can be found when we walk down a difficult road together.
